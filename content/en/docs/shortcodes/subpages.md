---
title: Subpages
---

Sometimes, [like here](/docs/shortcodes/), you want to show a list of the subpages of the page you are on. That is an ordinary markdown list:

```
- [Breadcrumbs](/docs/shortcodes/breadcrumbs/)
- [Button](/docs/shortcodes/button/)
- [Contact buttons](/docs/shortcodes/contactbuttons/)
```

In the documentation section you rarely need it: the `docs` brick already puts the whole tree in the sidebar, built from the pages themselves, and highlights the page you are on.
