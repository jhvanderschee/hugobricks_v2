---
title: Social buttons
---

If you want to link to social media you can use the social buttons shortcode. The links and their icons are defined in `data/<lang>/footer.yaml`. You can see them in action on the [elements page](/elements/) and on the [about page](/about/). To show the social buttons, write:

```
{{</* socialbuttons */>}}
```

It renders the same list the footer does, so the two never fall out of step.
