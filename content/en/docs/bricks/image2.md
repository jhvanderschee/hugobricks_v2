The alternative image brick is the [image](/docs/bricks/image/) brick mirrored: the picture comes first, the prose second, and the whole band is tinted.

## Use this brick

You do not ask for it — you get it. Image sections alternate automatically, so the second, fourth and sixth image brick on a page come out as the alternative face. Which phase a page starts on depends on what it opens with: after an `intro` the first band is tinted, after a full-bleed `header` the first image stays white.

The markdown is exactly that of the `image` brick:

```
---

## Trees in all their glory

Curabitur sit amet laoreet urna. Donec vel lobortis nunc.

![](/uploads/photos/hike/hike3.jpg){:.float}
```
